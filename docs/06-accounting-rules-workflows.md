# Accounting Rules and Workflows

## Debits and credits
- Assets and expenses normally increase with debits.
- Liabilities, equity, and revenue normally increase with credits.
- Every posted transaction must have equal total debits and credits.

## Journal entry workflow
1. Create draft journal.
2. Validate accounts and period.
3. Verify debit total equals credit total.
4. Post entry to general ledger.
5. Prevent edits after posting; use reversal entry for correction.

## Revenue recognition
- For cash-basis mode: recognize revenue when payment is received.
- For accrual-basis mode: recognize revenue when invoice is posted/sent depending company policy.
- Invoice posting: debit Accounts Receivable, credit Revenue, credit Tax Payable if tax applies.

## Expense recording
- Direct expense paid by cash/bank: debit Expense, debit Tax Paid/Recoverable if applicable, credit Cash/Bank.
- Vendor bill: debit Expense/Asset, credit Accounts Payable.

## Accounts receivable
- Invoice increases AR.
- Payment decreases AR when allocated.
- Overpayment creates customer credit liability or unapplied payment.

## Accounts payable
- Bill increases AP.
- Bill payment decreases AP and cash/bank.

## Tax handling
- Sales tax/VAT/GST collected is a liability until remitted.
- Input tax paid can be recorded as recoverable asset where applicable.
- Tax reports summarize collected, paid/recoverable, and net liability.

## Locked periods
- No create/post/edit/void of financial records dated inside locked period.
- Admin/accountant can unlock only with audit log and reason.

## Reversing entries
- Reversal duplicates original lines with debit/credit swapped.
- Reversal links to original journal entry.
- Original status becomes reversed or retains posted with reversal reference.

## Voiding invoices
- Draft invoice can be deleted/voided.
- Posted invoice must be voided through reversal journal, not hard-deleted.
- Paid invoice cannot be voided unless payment is unapplied/refunded first.

## Payment allocation
- Payments can be partially allocated across invoices.
- Allocation cannot exceed invoice outstanding balance.
- Unallocated balance remains customer credit.

## Bank reconciliation
- Imported transactions are matched to ledger entries.
- Reconciliation closes when statement ending balance equals cleared ledger balance.
- Adjustments require journal entry and audit reason.

## Financial statement generation
- P&L: revenue minus expenses for date range.
- Balance sheet: assets = liabilities + equity as of date.
- Cash flow: operating, investing, financing cash changes.
- Trial balance: debit/credit totals by account for period.
