# Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 1: Foundation
- Auth, companies, users, RBAC.
- Chart of accounts.
- Journal entries, posting, reversing.
- General ledger and trial balance.
- Audit logs.

## Phase 2: AR/Invoicing
- Customers, invoice creation, PDF generation, email sending.
- Payments received and allocations.
- AR aging and customer statements.

## Phase 3: AP/Expenses
- Vendors, bills, bill payments.
- Expenses, attachments, approvals.
- AP aging and vendor statements.

## Phase 4: Banking and Taxes
- Bank accounts, cash accounts, transfers.
- CSV import and reconciliation.
- Tax rates, tax groups, tax reports.

## Phase 5: Reporting and SaaS
- P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, exports.
- Subscription plans, usage limits, billing integration.
- Advanced dashboard analytics and notifications.

## Phase 6: Optional Inventory
- Products/services, stock movement, valuation, COGS.
